## Slide 3

Energy
Although we talk about energy being consumed or generated, energy is actually conserved.
This means that energy can flow into or out of a system, or it can change from one form to another, but it cannot be created or destroyed.
Energy associated with
macroscopic
objects, such as cars or baseballs, is known as
mechanical energy
. Mechanical energy comes in two forms:
Kinetic energy (energy of motion).
Potential energy (energy of position or configuration), which can take different forms (e.g., gravitational or electrical).
Work
and
heat
are two ways to transfer energy to an object (more about those later).

## Slide 4

Energy and Systems
It is often convenient to divide the universe into a
system
of interest and everything else, which is the environment of the system.
In addition to mechanical energy, a system can have
internal energy
(energy of random molecular motion or configuration).
Energy within the system can change from one form to another but the total energy in the system doesn’t change in the absence of energy transfer to or from the surroundings.

## Slide 6

Work
(2 of 2)
Only the component of force in the direction of the displacement contributes to work. Special cases:
A force applied to a stationary rigid object does no work on the object.
A force applied perpendicular to the displacement of an object does no work on the object.

## Slide 7

Work Can Be Positive or Negative
Work is positive if the force has a component in the same direction as the motion.
Work is negative if the force has a component opposite the direction of motion.
Work is zero if the force is perpendicular to the motion.
